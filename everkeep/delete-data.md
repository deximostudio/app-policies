# Delete your data — Everkeep

**App:** Everkeep  
**Developer:** Deximostudio

This page explains how to delete data associated with Everkeep.

## What Everkeep stores

Everkeep is local-first. Your journal (baby profiles, memories, photos, videos, voice notes, and settings) is stored **on your device**, not on Deximostudio servers.

When you purchase or restore **Everkeep Pro**, Apple or Google process the payment. We use **RevenueCat** to check Pro entitlement. That may involve purchase / entitlement information handled by those services — not your journal content.

## How to delete your data

### 1. Delete journal data on your device (primary)

Uninstall Everkeep from your device. That removes local app data for Everkeep on that device (subject to any device-level backups you keep, such as Android backup).

You can also clear the app’s storage in system settings before uninstalling, if you prefer.

There is no Everkeep cloud account. Deximostudio does not host your memories, so there is nothing for us to erase from our servers.

### 2. Purchase / entitlement records

Store purchases are managed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Deximostudio does not store your payment card details.

If you have a question about purchase or restore history related to Everkeep Pro:

1. Contact us using the link below  
2. Include your Play account email and approximate purchase date  
3. We will help where we can and point you to Google Play / RevenueCat processes when the data is held by those services

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Journal content on your device | Removed when you uninstall / clear app storage |
| Device backups (if enabled) | Managed by Google or your device maker; remove backups separately if needed |
| Play purchase records | Retained by Google under their policies |
| Entitlement checks (RevenueCat) | Subject to RevenueCat’s and the store’s retention policies |
