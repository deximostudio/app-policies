# Privacy Policy for Everkeep

**Last updated:** August 14, 2026  
**Developer:** Deximostudio  
**App:** Everkeep (`com.deximostudio.memoria` on Android; `com.deximostudio.memoria.Memoria` on iOS)

## Overview

Everkeep is a local-first baby memory journal for Android and iOS. We do not operate servers that store your memories, photos, videos, voice notes, or baby profiles. Your journal stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, sell, or share your journal content.**

Everkeep does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs that send your memories to Deximostudio. We do not receive your entries, media, or baby profile details on our servers.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

- Baby profiles (names, dates, photos, theme preferences)
- Memory entries (notes, media references, tags, favorites)
- Photos, videos, and audio you capture or import
- Optional place information you attach to an entry
- App settings (including app lock preferences)
- A local cache of Everkeep Pro purchase status (for offline use)
- Optional on-device transcription models you choose to download

## Permissions you may grant

Everkeep only uses permissions when needed for features you choose:

- **Camera and microphone** — to capture photos, video, or voice notes
- **Photo / media library** — to import media you select
- **Location** — only if you choose to attach a place to a memory
- **Biometrics / device credentials** — if you enable app lock
- **Network** — for optional model downloads and for purchase / restore with the app stores (see below)

You can deny optional permissions; core journaling still works without them where the platform allows.

## Purchases (Everkeep Pro)

Everkeep Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Billing). We use RevenueCat to check and restore entitlement status.

When you purchase or restore:

- Apple or Google processes the payment
- RevenueCat may receive purchase / entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features
- Your memories and media are not sent to RevenueCat or Deximostudio as part of purchase

Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies apply to store transactions.

## Optional network use

Most of Everkeep works offline. The app may use the network when you:

- Purchase or restore Everkeep Pro
- Download an optional on-device transcription model
- Open a link you choose (for example, this privacy policy)

Those requests do not upload your journal to Deximostudio.

## Sharing, export, and backup

Share, PDF export, and backup / restore are **user-initiated**. Files you export or share leave the app only through the system share sheet or paths you choose. Deximostudio does not receive those files.

## App lock

If you enable app lock, a PIN and/or device biometrics protect access on that device. Lock credentials stay on the device and are not sent to Deximostudio.

## Android backup

On Android, app data may be included in your device’s backup if backup is enabled. Backup content is managed by Google or your device manufacturer, not by Deximostudio.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes local app data from your device (subject to any device-level backups you keep). There is no Everkeep cloud account to delete because we do not host your journal.

## Children’s privacy

Everkeep is for parents and caregivers, not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
