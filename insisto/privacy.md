# Privacy Policy for Insisto

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026  
**Developer:** Deximostudio  
**App:** Insisto (`com.deximoapps.insisto` on Android)

## Overview

Insisto is a reminder app for Android that keeps reminding you until you mark a reminder done. Your reminders stay on your phone. Insisto has no internet permission: it cannot send your data anywhere, including to us.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, sell, or share any of your data.**

Insisto has no account, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it has no way to upload anything.

## Information stored on your device

To work, Insisto keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

- **Reminders:** the text you type, the time and date, how it repeats, how often it reminds you again, and the ring style
- **History:** when each reminder rang, how many times it reminded you, and when you marked it done, snoozed it or moved it to tomorrow
- **Settings:** default reminder interval, theme, and which setup steps you have completed
- **Purchase status:** a cached copy of your unlock, so it works offline

## Permissions you grant

Each permission is requested for one feature, and Insisto explains it before asking:

- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)**: to ring at the exact minute you chose, and to remind you again on time
- **Notifications**: to show the reminder and its Done, Snooze and Tomorrow buttons
- **Override Do Not Disturb** (optional, a notification setting you turn on): only for reminders you set to ring like an alarm
- **Run at startup**: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts
- **Vibration**: to vibrate when a reminder rings

Insisto does not use your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or Accessibility services.

## Backups and moving to a new phone

Insisto's data is excluded from Google cloud backup. When you set up a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy your reminders and settings to the new phone. The purchase is restored from Google Play.

## Export and import

**Settings > Export** saves your reminders to a file in a place you choose. **Settings > Import** reads such a file back. Files are created only when you ask, and Deximostudio does not receive them.

## Purchases (Insisto Unlock)

Insisto Unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and the purchase record. Insisto talks to the Google Play Store app on your phone to check it; your reminders are never part of that. Google's privacy policy applies to store transactions. Deximostudio does not receive your payment details.

## Fonts

Insisto may ask Google Play services on your phone for its typeface. Play services downloads the font file from Google; no reminder data is part of that request.

## Data deletion

Delete any reminder from its edit screen. Uninstalling Insisto removes all of its data from that phone. There is no Insisto account or server copy to delete.

See [Delete your data](delete-data.md) for details.

## Children's privacy

Insisto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
