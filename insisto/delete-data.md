# Delete your data — Insisto

**App:** Insisto  
**Developer:** Deximostudio

This page explains how to delete data associated with Insisto.

## What Insisto stores

Insisto stores everything **on your device**: your reminders, their history and your settings. It has no internet permission and no account, so Deximostudio holds none of your data.

When you purchase or restore **Insisto Unlock**, Google Play processes the payment and keeps the purchase record.

## How to delete your data

### 1. In the app

- Open a reminder and tap **Delete** to remove it and stop it ringing.
- Files you saved with **Settings > Export** live where you put them; delete them like any other file.

### 2. Uninstall

Uninstalling Insisto removes all of its data from that phone.

There is no Insisto cloud account, so there is nothing for us to erase from our servers.

### 3. Purchase records

Store purchases are managed by Google Play. Deximostudio does not store your payment details. For questions about an Insisto Unlock purchase, contact us with your Play account email and the approximate purchase date, and we will help or point you to Google Play.

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Reminders and their history | Removed one by one in the app, or all by uninstalling |
| Settings and cached purchase status | Removed by uninstalling |
| Exported files | Kept until you delete them |
| Play purchase records | Retained by Google under their policies |
