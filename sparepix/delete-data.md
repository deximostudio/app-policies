# Delete your data — SparePix

**App:** SparePix  
**Developer:** Deximostudio

This page explains how to delete data associated with SparePix.

## What SparePix stores

SparePix is local-first. Your photo index, similar groups, screenshot stacks, keep / delete marks, and settings are stored **on your device**, not on Deximostudio servers. Original photos stay in your Android gallery.

When you purchase or restore the **SparePix lifetime unlock**, Google Play processes the payment. That may involve purchase / entitlement information handled by Google — not your photos.

## How to delete your data

### 1. Delete photos on your device (primary)

In the app, review a similar group or screenshot month, confirm, and move items to **Android Trash / Recents** (or confirm delete on older Android). That is a gallery delete you control. SparePix does not upload those photos.

To remove SparePix’s local index and settings, uninstall SparePix. That removes local app data for SparePix on that device. Your remaining gallery photos stay in Google Photos / the system gallery unless you deleted them as above.

There is no SparePix cloud account. Deximostudio does not host your library, so there is nothing for us to erase from our servers.

### 2. Purchase / entitlement records

Store purchases are managed by Google Play. Deximostudio does not store your payment card details.

If you have a question about purchase or restore history related to the SparePix lifetime unlock:

1. Contact us using the link below  
2. Include your Play account email and approximate purchase date  
3. We will help where we can and point you to Google Play processes when the data is held by Google

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Local scan index, groups, and settings on your device | Removed when you uninstall SparePix |
| Photos you confirmed for Trash / delete | Follow Android Trash / Recents (or permanent delete); restore from Trash while the system still keeps them |
| Photos still in your gallery | Unchanged unless you confirmed delete |
| Play purchase records | Retained by Google under their policies |
