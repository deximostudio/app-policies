# Delete your data — Filetray

**App:** Filetray  
**Developer:** Deximostudio

This page explains how to delete data associated with Filetray.

## What Filetray stores

Filetray is local-first. Your scans, PDFs, folders, tags, search index, and OCR text are stored **on your device**, not on Deximostudio servers.

When you purchase or restore **Filetray Lifetime**, Google Play processes the payment. That may involve purchase / entitlement information handled by Google — not your documents.

## How to delete your data

### 1. Delete documents on your device (primary)

In the app, open a document and delete it. That removes that document’s pages, PDF, and OCR text from this device.

To remove the whole library, uninstall Filetray. That removes local app data for Filetray on that device.

There is no Filetray cloud account. Deximostudio does not host your papers, so there is nothing for us to erase from our servers.

### 2. Purchase / entitlement records

Store purchases are managed by Google Play. Deximostudio does not store your payment card details.

If you have a question about purchase or restore history related to Filetray Lifetime:

1. Contact us using the link below  
2. Include your Play account email and approximate purchase date  
3. We will help where we can and point you to Google Play processes when the data is held by Google

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Scans, PDFs, folders, tags, and OCR text on your device | Removed when you delete the document in-app or uninstall |
| Files you previously shared or saved to Files | Stay wherever you saved or sent them; delete those copies separately if needed |
| Play purchase records | Retained by Google under their policies |
