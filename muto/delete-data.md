# Delete your data — Muto

**App:** Muto  
**Developer:** Deximostudio

This page explains how to delete data associated with Muto.

## What Muto stores

Muto stores everything **on your device**: screen time history, the notification archive, limits and settings. It has no internet permission and no account, so Deximostudio holds none of your data.

When you purchase or restore **Muto Full**, Google Play processes the payment and keeps the purchase record.

## How to delete your data

### 1. In the app

- **Settings > Delete all data** removes usage history, the notification archive and every limit. Muto asks twice before deleting.
- Swipe a notification in the archive to delete just that one.
- **Settings > Auto-delete after** sets how long notifications are kept.

### 2. Uninstall

Uninstalling Muto removes all of its data from that phone.

There is no Muto cloud account, so there is nothing for us to erase from our servers.

### 3. Purchase records

Store purchases are managed by Google Play. Deximostudio does not store your payment details. For questions about a Muto Full purchase, contact us with your Play account email and the approximate purchase date, and we will help or point you to Google Play.

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Usage history, notification archive, limits | Removed by Delete all data or by uninstalling |
| Settings and cached purchase status | Removed by uninstalling |
| Play purchase records | Retained by Google under their policies |
