# Privacy Policy for Muto

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026  
**Developer:** Deximostudio  
**App:** Muto (`com.deximoapps.muto` on Android)

## Overview

Muto is a screen time, app limit and notification archive app for Android. Everything it records stays on your phone. Muto has no internet permission: it cannot send your data anywhere, including to us.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, sell, or share any of your data.**

Muto has no account, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it has no way to upload anything.

## Information stored on your device

To work, Muto keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

- **App usage:** how long you use each app per day, launches and phone unlocks, read from Android's usage statistics
- **Notification archive:** the app name, title, text and time of notifications you receive, so you can read them later and see how often you are interrupted
- **Limits:** the daily limits, time windows and modes you set
- **Settings:** archive retention, excluded apps, theme, and a hash of your limit PIN (never the PIN itself)
- **Purchase status:** a cached copy of your Muto Full unlock, so it works offline

Muto only lists apps that have a launcher icon. It does not read the full list of installed packages.

## Permissions you grant

Each permission is requested for one feature, and Muto explains it before asking:

- **Usage access**: to measure screen time per app and know when a limited app is open
- **Notification access**: to save notifications to the on-device archive and count interruptions
- **Display over other apps**: to show the pause screen when a limit is reached
- **Notifications**: for limit reminders and the status notification that keeps limits running
- **Run at startup**: to resume limits after the phone restarts

Muto does not use Accessibility services.

## Controlling notification data

Notification text can include personal messages and codes. You are in control of what Muto keeps:

- Exclude any app so its notifications are never stored
- Archived notifications are deleted automatically after a period you choose (30 days by default)
- Delete single notifications by swiping them away
- Ongoing notifications (music, navigation, downloads) are not stored

## Backups and moving to a new phone

Muto's data is excluded from Google cloud backup. When you set up a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy Muto's history and settings to the new phone. The purchase is restored from Google Play.

## Purchases (Muto Full)

Muto Full is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and the purchase record. Muto talks to the Google Play Store app on your phone to check it; your usage and notifications are never part of that. Google's privacy policy applies to store transactions. Deximostudio does not receive your payment details.

## Export

You can export daily usage totals as a CSV file. The export is created only when you ask, and saved where you choose. Deximostudio does not receive it.

## Data deletion

Use **Settings > Delete all data** to remove usage history, the notification archive and all limits from your phone. Uninstalling Muto also removes all of its data. There is no Muto account or server copy to delete.

See [Delete your data](delete-data.md) for details.

## Children's privacy

Muto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
