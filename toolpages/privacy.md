# Privacy Policy for ViaPDF

**Last updated:** September 10, 2026  
**Developer:** Deximostudio  
**App:** ViaPDF (`com.deximoapps.toolpages` on iOS)

## Overview

ViaPDF is an offline PDF toolkit for iOS. We do not operate servers that store your PDFs. Merge, split, compress, rotate, and OCR run on your device. Files you import stay on the device unless you choose to share or save them.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, sell, or share your PDFs or personal information.**

ViaPDF does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not receive your documents, OCR text, or file names on our servers.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

- PDFs you import or create, for as long as you keep them in the app or in Files
- Recents (security-scoped bookmarks to files you opened recently)
- App settings
- A local flag for the one free export
- A local cache of Full toolkit purchase status (for offline use)

## Permissions you may grant

ViaPDF only uses access you choose:

- **Files / document picker** — to open PDFs you select
- **Share Sheet / Save to Files** — when you export a result
- **Network** — for purchase / restore with the App Store (see below), and if you open a link such as this policy

There is no account, and the app does not request Camera, Photos, or Location.

## Purchases (Full toolkit)

Full toolkit is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple (App Store). StoreKit on your device checks and restores entitlement.

When you purchase or restore:

- Apple processes the payment
- Apple may receive purchase / entitlement information needed to unlock Full toolkit
- Your PDFs are not sent to Deximostudio as part of purchase

Apple’s privacy policy applies to store transactions.

## Optional network use

Core tools work offline. The app may use the network when you:

- Purchase or restore Full toolkit
- Open a link you choose (for example, this privacy policy)

Those requests do not upload your PDFs to Deximostudio.

## Sharing and export

Share, Save to Files, and copy-to-file are **user-initiated**. Files you export leave the app only through the system share sheet or paths you choose. Deximostudio does not receive those files.

On-device OCR (Apple Vision) reads text on your device so you can copy it. OCR text is not sent to us.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes local app data from your device (subject to any device-level backups you keep, such as iCloud Backup). There is no ViaPDF cloud account to delete because we do not host your files.

PDFs that already live in Files, iCloud Drive, or other apps are not deleted when you uninstall ViaPDF.

## Children’s privacy

ViaPDF is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
