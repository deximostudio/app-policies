# Delete your data — Soloclip

**App:** Soloclip  
**Developer:** Deximostudio

This page explains how to delete data associated with Soloclip.

## What Soloclip stores

Soloclip is local-first. Your clipboard history, pins, snippet folders, and settings are stored **on your device**, not on Deximostudio servers.

When you purchase or restore the **Soloclip unlock**, Google Play processes the payment. That may involve purchase / entitlement information handled by Google — not your clipboard contents.

## How to delete your data

### 1. Delete clipboard data on your device (primary)

In the app: **Settings → Wipe all data**. That deletes clips, pins, and folders stored on this device. There is no cloud copy.

You can also uninstall Soloclip. That removes local app data for Soloclip on that device.

There is no Soloclip cloud account. Deximostudio does not host your clips, so there is nothing for us to erase from our servers.

### 2. Purchase / entitlement records

Store purchases are managed by Google Play. Deximostudio does not store your payment card details.

If you have a question about purchase or restore history related to the Soloclip unlock:

1. Contact us using the link below  
2. Include your Play account email and approximate purchase date  
3. We will help where we can and point you to Google Play processes when the data is held by Google

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Clips, pins, and folders on your device | Removed when you wipe in Settings or uninstall |
| Files you previously exported or shared | Stay wherever you saved or sent them; delete those copies separately if needed |
| Play purchase records | Retained by Google under their policies |
